## 1. Core Pricing Model Overview

Meta WhatsApp Cloud API operates on a **24-hour conversation-based pricing model**, not a per-individual-message fee.

* When a conversation starts, a **24-hour window** opens.
* All messages sent within that 24-hour window under the same category incur **no additional charge**.
* Meta only charges for **successfully delivered** messages. If a phone number is inactive or undelivered, you are **not billed**.

## 3. Marketing Messages in Detail

### 3.1 What Qualifies as a Marketing Message?
Any message initiated by your business that promotes products, services, offers, or brand awareness to customers outside of an active 24-hour customer-service window.

### 3.2 Requirements for Sending Marketing Messages
1. **Pre-Approved Templates:** You cannot send custom free-text messages. Templates must be submitted and approved via the **WhatsApp Business Manager**.
2. **Opt-in Consent:** Recipients must have opted in or agreed to receive updates from your business.
3. **Opt-Out Option:** Marketing templates should include an opt-out button (e.g., `"Stop Promotions"` or `"Unsubscribe"`) to maintain a high phone number quality rating.

## 5. Daily Messaging Limits (Tiers)

Meta applies messaging tiers to prevent bulk spamming and protect user experience. Tiers determine how many **unique contacts** your business can message in a rolling 24-hour period:

| Tier Level | Daily Unique Contacts | How to Upgrade |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1 (Initial)** | 1,000 unique recipients / 24 hrs | Complete Meta Business Verification |
| **Tier 2** | 10,000 unique recipients / 24 hrs | Reach 50% of Tier 1 limit with High Quality rating within 7 days |
| **Tier 3** | 100,000 unique recipients / 24 hrs | Reach 50% of Tier 2 limit with High Quality rating |
| **Tier 4** | Unlimited recipients | Automatically unlocked with consistent high volume & quality |

> [!TIP]
> **Maintaining Phone Quality Rating:**
> * Keep your block and report rates minimal.
> * Avoid spamming contacts who haven't expressed interest.
> * Honor opt-out requests immediately.

## 6. How Billing & Payments Work in Meta

1. **Payment Setup:**
   * Go to **Meta Business Suite / Business Settings** ➔ **Billing & Payments** ➔ **Payment Methods**.
   * Add a valid Credit Card or international Debit Card with online transactions enabled.
2. **Billing Schedule:**
   * Meta operates on a **monthly threshold billing** cycle.
   * You are billed either when your account reaches a specific spending threshold (e.g., $25, $50, $100) or at the end of each billing cycle.
3. **Invoice Download:**
   * Monthly GST-compliant tax invoices can be downloaded directly from the **Meta Billing & Invoices** portal.
